## Classroom scenarios

- Grade 10 Newton: prediction → editable force → visible model → feedback works. It can anchor a demonstration, but AUD-001 makes the promised full-grade route unreliable.
- Grade 11 waves: incorrect numeric response gave an explanation; changing to standing-wave mode changed the displayed equation. Strong for conceptual contrast, but needs teacher prompts for graph interpretation.
- Grade 12 ideal gas: isochoric mode exposed final temperature; Revision Home generated a 10-question grade-12 test. Suitable for a short model demonstration, not yet enough evidence for a full assessment sequence.

## Academic sampling limits

The validators independently recompute declared numeric keys and check all 49 records structurally. That does not replace line-by-line curriculum review, textbook alignment, classroom piloting, or review by a Vietnamese physics teacher. Do not claim external academic validation.
