## Journey observations

- A began at Home, needed one primary-nav action to reach Learning, completed a Newton feedback/model/reload route, and retained a note. The helpful part is immediate feedback; the confusing part is the coexistence of legacy discovery and full-reader pathways.
- B entered a nonnumeric response for a numeric wave question and received an explanation. Switching the simulation to standing-wave mode changed the equation. Formula reading still needs a visual cue tied to the graph.
- C changed ideal-gas process, then opened grade-12 Revision Home. A 10-question non-duplicate test appeared. The learner cannot yet trust the home catalog to show every advertised full lesson (AUD-001).
- At 390×844 the sampled lesson had no horizontal document overflow and its table of contents worked. This is not a substitute for touch-device testing with an on-screen keyboard or safe-area hardware.

Top student improvements: fix complete-curriculum discovery; show “what next” after feedback/experiment; add visual annotations to formulas and graph-reading steps.
